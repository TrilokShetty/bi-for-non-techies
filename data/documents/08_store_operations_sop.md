# Store Operations SOP (Opening / Closing)

## Opening
1. Security disarm / CCTV check.
2. Cash float issue to tills with dual sign-off.
3. Shelf audit for overnight damage and expired food pulls.
4. Review stock-out dashboard for Grocery & Staples before peak footfall.

## Closing
1. Z-report and cash remittance to safe.
2. Lock high-value electronics accessories.
3. Temperature log for chillers.
4. Incident book entry for shrinkage or theft suspicion.

## Shrinkage & theft reporting
Any suspected theft: do not confront unsafely. Inform Security + Manager, preserve
CCTV clip IDs, file incident within 24 hours. Shrinkage above 1.5% of monthly
GMV (store) triggers Regional Ops review.

## Customer queues
Festival peaks: open additional UPI-only billing points where hardware allows.
