# Loyalty Programme Rules

## Tiers
| Tier | How customers typically qualify (demo) | Benefits (illustrative) |
|------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Silver | Default on signup | Earn points on delivered orders |
| Gold | Higher trailing 12-month spend | Bonus points days; priority support queue |
| Platinum | Top spend band | Early access to festive pre-sales |

## Points
Points accrue only on **delivered** orders net of returns. Cancelled orders do
not earn points. Returned lines claw back points within 14 days of return posting.

## Redemption
Points redeem at POS/app as discount_inr, never exceeding applicable MRP rules.
Points have no cash refund value.

## Privacy
Phone numbers in demo data are fake. In production, follow company privacy notice
and IT Act / SPDI handling per Legal guidance.
