# Payments & Settlement

## Accepted rails
In-store: UPI, debit/credit card, cash, wallet (where enabled).
App/web: UPI, cards, net banking, wallet, EMI (selected SKUs), COD (pin-code eligibility).

## UPI
Preferred for tickets under ₹2,000. Cashiers confirm success screen before
handing goods. Failed UPI → do not release goods; retry or alternate rail.

## COD cash remittance
Delivery partners remit COD cash per DC schedule. Store/DC posts remittance
same day. Customer refunds after COD returns follow Returns policy (UPI/bank,
not doorstep cash).

## Daily reconciliation
Cash office reconciles: cash tender, UPI acquirer report, card batch, wallet
settlement file, and POS Z-report. Breakages above ₹500 require manager note.

## Failed payments
If money is debited but order not confirmed, raise payment investigation within
4 hours. Provisional order hold max 24 hours pending gateway status.
