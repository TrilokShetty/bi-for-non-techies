# Pricing & MRP Compliance

## Core rule
**Never sell above the Maximum Retail Price (MRP)** printed on the pack under
Legal Metrology labelling requirements. POS must hard-block overrides above MRP.

## Discount authority
| Role | Max discount off MRP (non-festive) | Notes |
|------|-------------------------------------|-------|
| Cashier | 0% (scan promotions only) | |
| Floor supervisor | up to 5% | documented reason |
| Store manager | up to 15% | daily report |
| Regional merchandising | up to 25% | campaign codes only |
| Clearance / near-expiry | per Food Safety & Expiry policy | FEFO markdown ladder |

Bundled offers must still ensure the effective price of each MRP-marked item
does not exceed its MRP when allocated.

## Label basics (staff checklist)
- MRP inclusive of all taxes must be visible.
- Packer/manufacturer address and consumer care details as on pack.
- Date coding where applicable (packaged food).
- Do not sticker a higher price over MRP; promotional stickers may show offer
  price **below** MRP only.

## Channel consistency
App/web catalogue price must match in-store shelf price for the same SKU and
city pricing zone, except for clearly marked online-only deals.

## Audit
Mystery audits compare shelf tags, POS, and app. Variances above MRP are
critical findings and trigger immediate price correction plus incident report.
