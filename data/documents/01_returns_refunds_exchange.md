# Returns, Refunds & Exchange Policy

## Purpose
This policy explains how store and digital-channel teams handle customer returns,
exchanges, and refunds for merchandise sold by the Company across India
(in-store, app, and web).

## Eligibility window
- Most non-food categories: **7 calendar days** from delivery / purchase invoice date.
- Apparel and footwear (unworn, tags intact): **14 calendar days**.
- Packaged food and grocery staples: return only if **sealed and within best-before**,
  or if quality/expiry complaint is validated by the store manager.
- Electronics accessories: **7 days** if unused and original packaging intact.
  Opened earbuds/power banks may be exchange-only after QC.

## Non-returnable (illustrative)
- Personal-care items opened after purchase (hygiene).
- Innerwear and select personal products as tagged on the shelf label.
- Gift cards and promotional freebies with zero MRP.

## COD (Cash on Delivery) refund handling
COD is available on app and web for eligible pin codes.
When a COD order is returned after delivery:
1. Store/DC verifies the return and raises a refund ticket.
2. Refund is **not** paid as cash at the doorstep after return pickup.
3. Prefer refund to customer **UPI VPA** or **bank transfer** captured in the app profile.
4. Timeline target: **5–7 business days** after QC approval for UPI/bank;
   **7–10 business days** if only card instrument data is available from a prior prepaid attempt.
5. If the customer has no UPI/bank on file, issue **store credit** valid 12 months
   and note this in the grievance log.

## Prepaid refund timelines
- UPI: **1–3 business days** after QC approval.
- Debit/credit card: **5–7 business days** (issuer dependent).
- Wallet: **1–2 business days**.
- Net banking: **3–5 business days**.

## Exchange vs refund
Offer exchange first when the same SKU/size/colour is in stock at the originating
or nearest store. If unavailable, offer refund per payment method rules above.

## Documentation
Staff must retain: original invoice / e-invoice reference, return reason code
(damaged, wrong_item, quality, size, expired, changed_mind), photos for damaged
claims above ₹2,000, and manager approval for exceptions beyond the window.

## Escalation
Unresolved refunds beyond SLA → Customer Care L2 → Regional Ops → Legal only for
Consumer Protection Act related notices. See the grievance matrix document.
