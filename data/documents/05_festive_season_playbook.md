# Festive Season Planning Playbook

## Scope
Covers planning for major Indian festive peaks relevant to national retail:
Pongal/Sankranti, Holi, Eid windows, Onam, Navratri/Dussehra/Durga Puja,
Diwali cluster, Republic Day and Independence Day promotional weekends, and
end-of-season clearance.

## Stock build-up timelines (illustrative)
| Event | Start inbound build | Peak sell weeks | Focus categories |
|-------|---------------------|-----------------|------------------|
| Pongal / Sankranti | T-21 days | festival week | Grocery & Staples, Packaged Food |
| Onam | T-21 days | festival fortnight | Grocery & Staples (South bias) |
| Navratri / Durga Puja | T-30 days | 2–3 weeks | Apparel (ethnic), Home |
| Diwali | T-45 days | 3 weeks spanning Diwali | Electronics accessories, Home, Sweets (Packaged Food), Apparel |
| Republic / Independence sale | T-14 days | 4–5 day weekend | Electronics & Accessories, Apparel |

## Staffing & hours
Hypermarkets may extend hours to 11 pm on peak festive evenings with security
and cash-office coverage. Express stores follow mall rules where applicable.

## Promo calendar
National campaigns are published by Merchandising 6 weeks prior. Regional
overlays (e.g., stronger Onam in Kerala/Tamil Nadu, Durga Puja in East) are
approved by Regional Heads.

## After-action
Within 10 days post-peak: review sell-through, leftover ethnic wear markdown
plan, and COD return spikes from digital festive campaigns.
