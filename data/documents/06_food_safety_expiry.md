# Food Safety & Expiry Handling

## Labelling awareness (FSSAI-style, general)
Packaged food on shelf must show vegetarian/non-vegetarian marks, ingredients
where required, best-before or use-by dates, and manufacturer details as on pack.
Staff must not deface statutory labels.

## Near-expiry markdown ladder (demo SOP)
| Days to expiry | Action |
|----------------|--------|
| > 30 days | Normal sale |
| 16–30 days | Optional 10% markdown with shelf talker |
| 8–15 days | 20–30% markdown; FEFO enforced |
| 1–7 days | Deep markdown or pull to staff sale if approved |
| Past expiry | Remove immediately; do not sell |

## Customer complaints
Suspected unsafe food → isolate batch, record batch_code / expiry_date, notify
Store Manager and Quality. Refund/replace per Returns policy.

## Temperature-sensitive goods
Follow equipment logs for chillers. Downtime > 2 hours: Quality decision on
salvage vs discard.
