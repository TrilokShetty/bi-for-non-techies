# Customer Grievance & Escalation Matrix

## Principles
Handle complaints fairly and quickly. Align generally with the spirit of the
Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020
for digital orders — without quoting section numbers unless Legal provides them.

## Intake channels
Store desk, national care email/phone, in-app ticket. Every ticket gets a reason
code and SLA clock.

## Escalation ladder
| Level | Owner | Target response | Examples |
|-------|-------|-----------------|----------|
| L1 | Store / Care agent | same day | refund status, exchange |
| L2 | Customer Care Lead | 48 hours | repeated refund delay, COD disputes |
| L3 | Regional Operations Manager | 5 business days | multi-store pattern issues |
| L4 | Compliance / Legal | as advised | formal legal notices, regulator queries |

## Unresolved complaint path
If a customer says a complaint is unresolved after L2 SLA, escalate to **L3 Regional
Operations Manager**. Care agents must share the ticket ID and not promise
settlement amounts beyond policy.

## E-commerce specific
For app/web orders: display company grievance contact in the invoice footer
template; preserve order logs for the retention period Finance defines.
